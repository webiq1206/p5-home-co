# Same-door replacement labor correction

Release 2026-10-02.8 synthetic estimate P5-5F4F0AB1 priced removing three existing passage levers and installing three owner-supplied replacements on the same three doors as two PB-08-71-01 labor charges. The rate is **Door hardware install / replace, per door (labor)**. The saved mapping retained 3 × $70 twice, plus a $75 shared consumables allowance: $495 direct cost. The provider audit accepted both charges. No customer email or phone was supplied.

The existing deterministic correction missed plural “levers” and the source's “same three doors” wording. Release .9 recognizes that exact relationship, requiring one complementary pair with identical quantity, range, factor, unit, rate amount, building and floor. Ambiguous groups, different doors or hardware, additional hinge work, hazardous disposal and independently priced removal remain separate. The retained replacement labor explicitly describes removal and installation. Small lever disposal receives explicit coverage through the existing owner-authorized shared allowance, not free coverage from a labor-only rate. The saved fixture becomes $210 labor + $75 shared allowance = $285 direct cost.

Customer copy also claimed consumables were part of labor. Only affirmative generated statements of that form are reconciled when retained positive labor-only lines and a positive policy allowance explicitly covering consumables support the correction. Original scope, raw audit, unresolved issues, negations and warnings remain intact. Rendering copy alone does not change prices. The duplicate-charge diagnostic itself is unchanged.

The synthetic fixture preserves the original reviewed scope, mapping, resolution, audit and finance snapshot without contact details. Before the fix, both primary regressions failed and ten separation cases passed. Focused tests now include full saved-fixture pricing, correction idempotence, copy-only price invariance, absent/zero/unsupported allowance cases and preservation of genuinely separate removal. No live provider calls were used to develop the correction. P5 remains isolated; no child-brand rollout or PR84 code is included.
