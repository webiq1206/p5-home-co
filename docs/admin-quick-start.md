# Using the lead manager

One page. This is everything you need.

## The five steps

1. **Open Lead Manager** from the menu at the top of the panel. That is the
   Needs Your Attention board. (The panel opens on a dashboard that summarises
   it, alongside the company's finances.)
2. **Start at the top.** The first lead is the most urgent one.
3. **Do the recommended action.** Tap Call, Text, or Email.
4. **Log what happened.** Pick the outcome that matches.
5. **Confirm the next action and its date.** Then move to the next lead.

That is the whole job. If the board is empty, you are done.

## What the groups mean

| Group | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Nobody has responded and it is badly overdue. Do these first. |
| **Needs response** | Waiting on us. A reply, an owner, or an overdue action. |
| **Due today** | Something is scheduled for today. |
| **Waiting on customer** | We have done our part. No action needed. |
| **Upcoming** | Scheduled for later, or snoozed. |
| **Recently completed** | Closed in the last few days. |

## Reading a card

Every card tells you **why it is there** in a plain sentence, such as
"No one has contacted this lead yet. 12 business minutes since it arrived."
You never have to work it out from a colour.

It also shows the client, which P5 brand it belongs to, the project type,
where the property is, who owns it, the stage, and the next action.

## Rules worth knowing

- **A lead that arrives outside business hours is not late.** Its clock starts
  at 7:00am on the next working day. We work Monday to Saturday, 7:00am to
  6:00pm.
- **The response clock stops when a person reaches out** — even a voicemail.
  An automatic email does not count.
- **Snoozing needs a reason and a date.** Snoozing never hides a lead that has
  gone past its response deadline.
- **You cannot finish a lead without logging the outcome** and confirming what
  happens next, unless the deal is closed.

## If something looks wrong

Tell an administrator. Do not create a second lead for the same person — if
they enquired twice, the system already attached it to the existing one.
