# P5 independent pricing evidence

These references were consulted September 30, 2026. They have not been promoted into the approved owner catalog and do not establish acceptance of a P5 estimate.

## Local labor context

The [BLS Boise City May 2025 release](https://www.bls.gov/regions/west/news-release/occupationalemploymentandwages_boisecity.htm), published June 26, 2026, reports the following mean employee hourly wages. Its metropolitan area includes Ada, Boise, Canyon, Gem and Owyhee counties; it excludes self-employed workers.

| Occupation | Mean wage, USD/hour |
| --- | ---: |
| Carpenters | 27.09 |
| Construction laborers | 23.52 |
| Electricians | 31.67 |
| Plumbers, pipefitters and steamfitters | 28.66 |
| Tile and stone setters | 24.38 |
| Painters | 22.21 |

These observations describe wages, not installed-unit costs, loaded payroll or customer billing rates. Any comparison to P5 must separately establish crew composition, productivity, payroll burden, equipment, travel/minimums and subcontractor-versus-employee cost basis. No conversion factor or project productivity is supplied by this table.

## Fees and jurisdiction

The [City of Boise fee page](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/planning-and-development-services/fees/) and official [October 2023 building fee document](https://www.cityofboise.org/media/17652/final-boise-city-building-code-fee-schedule-10-1-23.pdf) provide a valuation-based building-permit schedule. The document lists residential plan review at 20% of the building-permit fee. Its age and exact applicability must be confirmed for a specific estimate; retrieval does not establish that every fee remains applicable. It does not supply a complete all-in permit budget for every jurisdiction or project.

The City's [impact-fee page](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/finance/impact-fees/) currently links the [FY2026 schedule](https://www.cityofboise.org/media/20458/fy-2026-impact-fee-schedule_final.pdf), effective October 1, 2025. Residential fees vary by dwelling size and planning area, with a stated 1% surcharge. The PDF's extracted text separates some area labels from their tables, so an amount must not be assigned to an address from text order alone. Confirm the planning area and applicable exemptions/credits using the City's calculator or project assessment. New-home and ADU cases must distinguish these fees from building, trade, utility and design charges and from any assembly price that already includes them.

## Acceptance boundary

Public wage tables, permit schedules and builder marketing ranges cannot replace an equivalent itemized bid. The qualification suite still needs dated scope-matched supplier/trade quotes or completed-project actuals, including quantities, specifications, locality and exclusions. Reference tolerances must be defined before viewing the estimator output. No numerical market-accuracy pass is recorded from these sources.

Two safeguards are implemented in the new compiler: owner catalog rates retain their actual recorded date and existing review interval, and missing/future dates cannot acquire a fabricated current timestamp. This prevents false freshness; it does not verify the underlying amount.
